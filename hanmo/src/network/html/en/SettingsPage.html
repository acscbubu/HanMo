AirNote

Type and save your text here, then read it like a book on the X4 Pro under Apps → AirNote.

    One item per line, up to 64 items, each up to 240 bytes. Tick items below or pick an item's target position, then press save; you can also tick items on the X4 Pro itself by long-pressing an item and choosing its target position. [x] means done, [ ] means not done.

    Notes are stored as UTF-8 TXT files in /AirNote on the SD card, up to 16 KiB each. The on-device web page has no login protection; do not keep private data on it on an untrusted network.

    AirPhoto

    Pick photos from this computer or phone. They are converted here in your browser into the bitmap format the X4 Pro draws, then sent to the device over the same upload connection the file manager uses.

    Cropping to fill trims the photo to the panel's shape, so every upload is the same size. The whole photo is never trimmed, but leaves blank edges when the shapes differ.

    Floyd-Steinberg suits photos; None suits plain text and line art.

    100 for both means no adjustment. Four gray levels leave little headroom, so send one photo at 100 first and nudge from there.

    Photos on the X4 Pro

    Each photo sends the original into /AirPhoto, two display bitmaps for portrait and landscape into /AirPhoto/.view, and one 120x160 thumbnail into /AirPhoto/.thumb. All three bitmaps are 4-level grayscale with Floyd-Steinberg dithering, which is what the e-ink panel can show. Photos go one after another; keep this page open until the list says every photo is done.

    JPEG/PNG originals wider than 2048 pixels or taller than 3072 pixels can still upload with browser-generated previews, but the X4 Pro cannot decode their originals to set wallpaper. Copies imported directly to the SD card at these dimensions cannot generate a preview cache on the device. Resize them before sending if you need those features.
